Logical Operators with Symbols:

1-Conjunction (AND) (∧): Both friends must have tickets to get in.

2-Disjunction (OR) (∨): At least one of the two friends must have a ticket.

3-Negation (NOT) (¬): Reverses the light state (if on, turns it off, and vice versa).

4-Implication (IF...THEN) (→): If you have a ticket, you get in; if not, it doesn’t matter.

5-Bi-conditional (IF AND ONLY IF) (↔): Both must have tickets or neither must have tickets.

6-Exclusive OR (XOR) (⊕): Exactly one of the two friends must have a ticket.
